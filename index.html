IT support that answers, fixes and keeps watch.

Informed Technology Services provides managed IT support for small and mid-sized businesses: a help desk your staff can email or text, device and Microsoft 365 management, security monitoring and remote support.

What we do

Help desk

Email, chat or text us a problem and get clear steps back, with a technician one reply away.

Devices and Microsoft 365

Setup, updates, user accounts, licences and the day-to-day administration of your workstations and cloud services.

Security monitoring

Endpoint protection, patching and alerts for the things that should never go unnoticed.

Remote support

With your permission, a technician connects to your computer and fixes the problem while you watch.

Text message notifications

Our help desk can send text messages to our technicians and, when a client asks for it, to the client's staff: ticket alerts, status updates and reminders. Message frequency varies with your tickets. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help.

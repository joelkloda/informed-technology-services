Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

Informed Technology Services ("we", "us") provides IT support services to businesses. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you contact our help desk or use our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, work email address, phone number and the company you work for.
  • Support requests: the content of the emails, chat messages, text messages and calls you send to our help desk, including screenshots and files you attach.
  • Device information: for computers we manage, technical details such as the device name, installed software, updates and security status.
  • Text-message consent: whether you have asked to receive text messages from us, and when.

How we use it

  • To answer and resolve your support requests and keep a record of the work done.
  • To send notifications about your requests by email, chat or text message, if you have chosen to receive them.
  • To manage, secure and update the devices and accounts we look after for your company.
  • To bill the company we have a service agreement with.

Text messages (SMS)

If you opt in to text messages, we use your mobile number only to send you messages about your support requests and our services: ticket alerts, status updates and reminders. You can stop at any time by replying STOP to any message; reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories of information above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run our help desk (for example our email, messaging and hosting providers), and only for that purpose; with your own company, which is our client; or when the law requires it.

Retention and security

Support records are kept for as long as we provide services to your company and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records. Access is limited to our technicians, protected by sign-in with two-step verification, and our systems are kept patched and monitored.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you can withdraw text-message consent at any time (reply STOP). Contact us at [contact email].

Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
